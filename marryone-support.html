Help & Support

Help with your MarryOne account, profile, conversations, privacy, and Premium membership.

Common questions

How do I update my profile?

Open My Profile in the MarryOne app to edit available information. Some changes may need moderation approval before other members can see them.

Why aren't my profile details visible?

Submitted revisions may remain pending until reviewed. Public, Members Only, and Private visibility settings also determine who can see approved information.

How do Likes and messages work?

Free members can send up to three outgoing messages per conversation in total. Without a mutual match, Free recipients cannot read incoming messages. With an active mutual match, Free recipients may read the first three incoming messages. Premium provides expanded authorized messaging, subject to blocking and safety rules.

How do I manage or cancel Premium?

Apple handles App Store subscriptions. Manage or cancel through your Apple account's Subscriptions settings. Use Restore Purchases in MarryOne if an existing eligible purchase is not showing.

How do I block or report someone?

Use the available Block or Report controls in the app. Do not share sensitive personal information unnecessarily.

How do I delete my account?

Use the Delete Account option in MarryOne Settings and follow the confirmation steps. Deleting an account does not automatically cancel an App Store subscription.

What if I can't sign in?

Check your sign-in method and email access. Never share authentication codes with anyone.
