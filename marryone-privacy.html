1. Overview

MarryOne is an adults-only matrimonial platform operated by Anjaan Games & Apps Studios. This policy describes the information involved in accounts, approved member profiles, discovery, communication, safety, and Premium membership. Information visibility depends on applicable settings, eligibility, moderation, and account restrictions.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on what you choose to provide or use, information may include your name, verified email address, account identifier, profile photos and posts, biography, marriage intentions, relationship status, interests, education, profession, lifestyle and optional profile details. Optional fields may contain sensitive personal information if you choose to submit it. We also process Likes, matches, messages, profile interactions, reports, moderation records, and subscription entitlement status. Technical and security information needed to operate and protect the service may also be processed.

3. Accounts and Age Eligibility

MarryOne is intended for people aged 18 and older. Registration requires an adult declaration. An adult declaration is not the same as government-ID age verification. Account authentication uses supported sign-in methods and related account information.

4. Profile Visibility

Approved profile content can have different visibility settings. Public content may be available to eligible members. Members Only content requires the applicable Premium entitlement. Private content is intended for the account owner. Blocking, moderation, and eligibility rules can further restrict access. Saved changes awaiting approval are not necessarily visible to other members.

5. Photos and Posts

Photos and posts submitted to MarryOne may undergo moderation before publication. Approved content is shown according to its audience settings and applicable restrictions. Rejected or pending content is not automatically public. Historical media or related moderation records may remain subject to security and retention rules.

6. Likes, Matches, and Messages

MarryOne processes Likes, mutual matches, and text conversations to provide member communication. Free members have a limited outgoing-message allowance per conversation. Incoming-message visibility depends on mutual-match status or Premium authorization, as well as blocking and moderation. Messages are not represented as end-to-end encrypted. Authorized safety processes may access information where permitted by applicable policies and law.

7. How Information Is Used

We use information to provide accounts and profiles, enable member discovery and communication, enforce visibility and subscription permissions, operate moderation and support processes, protect the platform, troubleshoot issues, and comply with legal obligations. We do not describe MarryOne as an advertising-tracking service; any analytics or third-party processing must be separately disclosed if enabled.

8. Service Providers

MarryOne uses infrastructure and service providers to operate authentication, data storage, application services, and Apple in-app purchases. Providers may process information as needed for these functions, subject to their respective responsibilities. A final published policy should accurately identify any additional processors or external services used in the release build.

9. Premium and Purchases

Premium subscriptions are offered through Apple's in-app purchase system. Apple handles payment processing; MarryOne uses purchase and entitlement information to determine access to Premium features. Subscription management and cancellation are handled through the applicable Apple account controls. Account deletion does not itself cancel an active App Store subscription.

10. Safety, Reports, and Moderation

Members can use available blocking and reporting features. Authorized reviewers may examine submitted profile content, photos, reports, and relevant safety records to enforce platform rules. Moderation decisions and audit information may be retained for accountability and security. Access should be limited to authorized personnel and legitimate purposes.

11. Data Security

MarryOne uses access controls and server-side authorization designed to restrict access to member information. No online system can guarantee absolute security. Content marked Private is not a promise of end-to-end encryption or absolute protection from authorized processing required to operate the service.

12. Retention and Account Deletion

Members can initiate account deletion through the available in-app flow. Deletion removes or restricts account information according to the service's implemented deletion process. Some information may be retained where needed for security, moderation audits, legal obligations, dispute handling, or backups. This draft does not assert an unverified fixed retention period or immediate deletion of every backup.

13. Your Choices and Privacy Rights

Members can manage supported profile visibility settings, update eligible profile information, block or report members, manage their subscriptions, and request account deletion. Additional privacy rights may apply depending on location. Requests can be directed through the verified support channel, subject to identity and applicable legal requirements.

14. Policy Updates and Contact

We may update this policy as features or data practices change. Material changes should be communicated as required. For privacy questions or support, use the official Anjaan Games & Apps Studios support page. The final public version should include the verified contact details used in the approved in-app policy.
