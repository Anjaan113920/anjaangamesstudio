About

Studio overview

Anjaan Games Studio creates fun, replayable, and easy-to-pick-up mobile games for iOS. The studio focuses on arcade, strategy, and puzzle titles built solo in Unity, with an emphasis on simple controls, clean design, and steady improvements over time.
