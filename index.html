Anjaan Games Studio

Anjaan Games Studio creates polished and accessible mobile experiences for iOS. Every app is independently developed in Unity by Amit Kandial, from the first gameplay idea through development, testing, App Store publishing, and future updates.

The studio explores different genres including arcade, strategy, board games, logic puzzles, educational experiences, and useful mobile tools.
